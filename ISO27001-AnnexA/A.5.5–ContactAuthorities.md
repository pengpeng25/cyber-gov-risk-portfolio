# A.5.5 – Contact with Authorities

**Clause:** ISO/IEC 27001, A.5.5  
**Title:** Contact with Authorities

---

## One-Sentence Takeaway
> ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.5 requires organizations to maintain formal, authorized contact with relevant authorities to support compliance, incident response, and risk management.

---

## Purpose
Ensure the organization can **communicate effectively and appropriately with external authorities** to prevent, manage, and respond to information security incidents and regulatory requirements.

---

## Key Requirements
* Identify **relevant authorities** (regulators, law enforcement, service providers)
* Establish and maintain **formal contact arrangements**
* Define procedures for:
  * Approvals
  * NDAs
  * Reporting formats and timelines
  * Authorized communication channels

---

## Operational Considerations
* A **designated liaison officer** is recommended to:
  * Maintain relationships with authorities
  * Receive early warnings and updates
  * Coordinate communication during incidents
* Contacts may be:
  * **Event-driven** (incident reporting)
  * **Time-driven** (scheduled regulatory communications)

---

## Audit Expectations
Auditors will verify:
* Relevant contacts are **identified, documented, and up to date**
* Contact details appear in **business continuity and support documentation**
* Organization understands applicable **legal and regulatory requirements**
* Information-sharing arrangements are **authorized and controlled**
* Communication with authorities is **appropriate, timely, and authorized**
