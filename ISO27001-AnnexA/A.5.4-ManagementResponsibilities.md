# A.5.4 – Management Responsibilities

**Clause:** ISO/IEC 27001, A.5.4  
**Title:** Management Responsibilities

---

## One-Sentence Takeaway
> ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.4 ensures that management actively enforces and supports information security by requiring personnel to comply with established policies and controls.

---

## Purpose
The purpose of management responsibilities is to ensure that **information security policies and controls are actively enforced, supported, and applied** throughout the organization.

---

## Key Responsibilities of Management
* Require personnel to:
  * Follow information security policies
  * Apply topic-specific policies and procedures
* Demonstrate:
  * Visible commitment to information security
  * Leadership by example
* Reinforce the message that:
  * Information security is **part of everyone’s job**
  * Security efforts are **valued and recognized**

---

## Operational Role of Management
* Managers are responsible for:
  * Monitoring compliance during daily work
  * Detecting deviations from policies and procedures
  * Initiating corrective actions when necessary
* Effective oversight does **not require heavy policing**—awareness enables early detection.

---

## Audit Expectations
Auditors will assess whether:
* Managers:
  * Understand their information security responsibilities
  * Actively enforce compliance
* Evidence exists of:
  * Security being discussed in meetings
  * Awareness activities (e.g., phishing tests)
  * Remedial training for non-compliance
  * Motivation or reward mechanisms
* Senior staff:
  * Follow the same rules as other employees
  * Do not receive informal exemptions
