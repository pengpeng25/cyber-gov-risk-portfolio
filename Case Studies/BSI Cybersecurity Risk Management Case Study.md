# BSI Cybersecurity Risk Management Case Study

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Cybersecurity Risk Management Program** for Business Supplies, Inc. (BSI), a regional logistics organization transitioning into a digital-first enterprise.

The objective was to transform an informal, reactive security posture into a structured **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) operating model** aligned with the ISO/IEC 27001 international standard.

The engagement demonstrates applied risk assessment, governance design, executive reporting, and remediation planning within a mid-market enterprise context.

---

## The Problem: “Growth Outpacing Governance”

BSI’s operational expansion and digital transformation outpaced its cybersecurity maturity, resulting in several critical gaps:

- **Reactive Security Posture**  
  No dedicated security personnel and no formal Information Security Policy (ISP).

- **Infrastructure Technical Debt**  
  Critical identity and financial systems (e.g., AD SQL DB, TAS systems) operating on aging, end-of-life hardware.

- **Compliance Exposure**  
  No formal audit validation for:
  - PCI payment data  
  - Sensitive employee records (PII / medical-related data)

These conditions elevated the organization’s exposure to financial disruption, regulatory penalties, and reputational damage.

---

## Solution & Framework: ISO/IEC 27001

ISO/IEC 27001 was selected to establish a formal **Information Security Management System (ISMS)**.

This framework was chosen for its:

- **Risk-Based Governance Model**  
  Ensures investment targets high-impact risk areas rather than reactive spending.

- **Executive Accountability Structure**  
  Formal assignment of risk ownership and required management review cycles.

- **Certification Potential**  
  Supports auditable assurance for high-value contracts and enterprise partnerships.

The implementation shifts cybersecurity from an IT support function to an enterprise governance function.

---

## Methodology & Key Artifacts

### Weighted Asset Prioritization

A quantitative scoring model was developed to evaluate **23 technical assets**, which were consolidated into **8 executive-level business bundles**.

**Weighted Criteria:**

- Revenue Impact – 45%  
- Legal / Regulatory Liability – 35%  
- Cost of Protection – 10%  
- Recovery Complexity – 10%

**Top-Ranked Asset:**  
**TERP-Web (E-commerce Platform)** due to high PCI exposure and direct revenue dependency.

This model ensured risk prioritization aligned with business impact rather than technical preference.

---

### Threat Landscape Analysis

Threats were ranked using weighted evaluation based on:

- Likelihood  
- Economic Impact  
- Current Vulnerability  
- Detection Difficulty  

Primary enterprise threats identified:

- **T1 – Information Extortion (Ransomware)**  
- **T3 – Human Error (Phishing / Social Engineering)**  

The analysis highlighted human vulnerability and infrastructure obsolescence as primary risk drivers.

---

###  Risk Heat Map (Likelihood × Impact)

A quantitative **Risk Matrix (L × I)** identified multiple “Red Zone” exposures (Score = 25), indicating business-critical risk concentrations.

Critical intersections included:

- **Human Error × Banking Data**  
- **Obsolescence × Identity Infrastructure (AD SQL DB)**  

These represent high-likelihood, high-impact scenarios capable of causing operational shutdown or financial compromise.

---

## 12-Month Mitigation Roadmap

A phased remediation plan was designed to reduce red-zone exposure by more than **40% within the first year**.

### Q1 – Governance Foundation
- Security Awareness Training  
- Formal Information Security Policy development  

### Q2 – Infrastructure Stabilization
- Replacement of legacy Server A & B systems  
- Patch and lifecycle management formalization  

### Q3 – Data Resilience
- Modernization and encryption of BARS backup system  
- Backup testing and ransomware recovery planning  

### Q4 – Operationalization
- Incident Response Plan development  
- Internal compliance audit and executive reporting cycle  

This roadmap transitions BSI from reactive IT management to continuous risk governance.
